### Building at the intersection of medicine, intelligence, and action

I work on engineering systems that connect medical perception, decision-making, and real-world execution. My interests span surgical intelligence, medical image computing, intelligent agents, and the tools that make research and technical work more reliable.

- **Surgical AI & medical imaging** — segmentation, 3D understanding, surgical-scene perception, and image-guided workflows.
- **Robotics & planning** — translating perception and task context into safe, useful action.
- **Agents & developer tools** — knowledge systems, technical workflows, and agent-assisted engineering.
